## About Me

I am a Computer Science Engineering undergraduate at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham with a strong focus on Machine Learning and Deep Learning, grounded in mathematical rigor and systems-level thinking.

My work centers on understanding how linear algebra, probability theory, optimization, and computational constraints fundamentally shape modern learning algorithms. I am particularly interested in designing and analyzing deep neural architectures — including CNNs and Transformers — through the lens of representation learning, structured modeling, and scalable computation.

My core interests include:
- Deep Learning architectures and optimization dynamics  
- Representation learning and high-dimensional feature modeling  
- Computer Vision and Natural Language Processing  
- Time-Series modeling and sequential learning  
- Efficient training pipelines and scalable ML systems  
- End-to-end production deployment of AI systems  

I aim to bridge theory and implementation — developing mathematically principled models and translating them into efficient, robust, and production-ready machine learning systems.
